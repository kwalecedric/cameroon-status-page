About this project

Why this exists and who built it

The problem

Every day, millions of Cameroonians use Sara Money, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, Express Union and others to pay bills, send money, and run businesses. When these services fail, users have no way to know whether the problem is on their phone, their network, or the provider's server. This creates panic, distrust, and financial loss.

The solution

Cameroon Fintech Status monitors the major financial services in Cameroon and provides a single, clear view of what is working and what is not — in real time. No more guessing. No more panic.

Built by

This platform was designed and built by Ngong Kwale Cedric, a self-taught developer from Cameroon, as part of a broader effort to bring transparency and trust to digital financial services in Central Africa.
